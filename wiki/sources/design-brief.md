# Visual Design Brief (summary)

Original: `.raw/migrated/design-brief.md`.

## Posture

Light, polished B2B SaaS. Calm, trustworthy, operational, focused on clarity over marketing spectacle. Premium but not flashy.

## Hard rules

- **Inter** font throughout.
- App background `#f3f4f6`; cards white.
- Indigo accent `#6366f1` (hover `#4f46e5`).
- 8–12px radii, thin borders, subtle shadows.
- Desktop-first ~1440px.
- Top bar pre-project; left sidebar (~240px) project-level.
- AI content must be inspectable, not magical.

## Avoid

Dark mode. Glassmorphism. Neon gradients. Consumer-social aesthetics. Empty dashboard filler.
